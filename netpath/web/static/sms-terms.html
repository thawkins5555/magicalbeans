SappiWhere

SMS Terms

1. Program

SappiWhere is Sappi's self-hosted network monitoring platform. The alert text program sends automated SMS notifications about network events — a device or service down, a path degrading, a matching trap or syslog message, an IP conflict, a mass outage — and a follow-up when the condition clears, to Sappi IT staff and contractors who administer the monitored network.

2. Eligibility and consent

Only holders of a SappiWhere login can enrol, and only for their own number, from the Account dialog: enter the number, read the consent notice, tick both boxes (one accepting these Terms and the Privacy Policy, one consenting to the alert texts), receive a one-time verification code by text and enter it (double opt-in). No texts are sent to a number that has not completed this step.

3. Message frequency and cost

Messages are event-driven and vary with network activity, capped per hour by the administrator. Message and data rates may apply per the recipient's mobile plan. Sappi does not charge for the program.

4. Opting out

Reply STOP to any message, or press Stop texts on the Account dialog. A stop is honoured immediately and recorded. To resume, re-verify your number on the Account dialog; if you stopped by texting STOP, reply START first so your carrier lifts its block. A START reply on its own does not resume texts.

5. Help

Reply HELP to any message, or contact Thomas Hawkins in Sappi IT.

6. Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

7. Changes

Sappi may update these terms. The current version is always at this address. Continued enrolment after a change is acceptance.

8. Contact
