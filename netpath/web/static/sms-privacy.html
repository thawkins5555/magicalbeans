SMS Privacy

1. What is collected

The mobile number entered, the time consent was given, the time the number was verified, any opt-out and how it happened (Stop texts, or a STOP reply), and a log of each alert text sent (number, text, time, delivered or not). The verification code itself is stored only as a one-way hash and never in a log.

2. How it is used

Solely to send the alert texts described in the SMS Terms and to prove consent and opt-out.

3. Sharing

Messages are delivered through Twilio, Sappi's SMS provider, which receives the number and message content to deliver them. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Consent and opt-in data are not sold, shared or transferred to any other party.

4. Where it is kept

In the SappiWhere database on Sappi's own server, alongside the user's account, readable only by that user and by a SappiWhere administrator. Changes are logged.

5. Retention

Kept while the account exists, deleted when a SappiWhere administrator removes the account. A stopped number's record is kept as evidence of the opt-out until then.

6. Your choices

Stop texts at any time (STOP or the Account dialog). Ask Thomas Hawkins in Sappi IT to remove the number.

7. Contact

Thomas Hawkins, Sappi IT — through your usual Sappi IT support channel.
